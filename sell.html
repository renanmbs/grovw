The listing process

Four things that decide
what your home sells for.

None of them are luck. Each one is a decision made before your home ever reaches a buyer.

01 · Preparation

What we do before a single photograph

We walk the house together and sort the work into three piles: what returns more than it costs, what has to be disclosed, and what you can safely ignore. Most sellers spend money on the wrong pile.

Paint, light, landscaping and decluttering are usually the whole list. You will get it in writing, with the cost next to each line, before anything is booked.

02 · Strategic pricing

Priced to the market, not to a hope

A list price is a position, not a wish. We build it from closed sales in your own subdivision, what is live against you right now, and how long those have been sitting.

Overpricing costs more than it ever gains: the first two weeks are the only ones with a fresh audience, and a price cut later tells every buyer to wait for the next one.

03 · Professional marketing

Photographed and presented properly

Professional photography, a floor plan, and copy that reads like it was written by someone who had been in the house. The listing goes to the MLS, the syndicated portals and our own channels on the same day.

Everything is bilingual. A meaningful share of Utah buyers search in Spanish, and most listings quietly ignore them.

04 · Experienced guidance

A broker on the file, not a hand-off

You get Jonathan Perez, the broker-owner, on the negotiation and on the contract — not a coordinator you meet after you have signed.

Offers, inspection requests, appraisal gaps and deadlines are where a sale is actually won or lost. That is the part we do not delegate.
