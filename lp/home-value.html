Why an agent's number beats an automated one

Automated estimates work from tax records and square footage. They cannot see the things that actually move your price.

  • A finished basement that was never permitted, so it never made the county record
  • The remodel you did in 2023 that no public database knows about
  • Which of your neighbors' sales were cash, distressed, or between family — and should be thrown out of the comparison
  • What buyers in your specific neighborhood are actually paying over or under asking right now
  • Whether your street's inventory means you should list this month or wait for spring

You'll get a written range with the comparable sales it's based on, so you can check the reasoning yourself.
