Own in Ogden sets its assistance by profession. These are the program's own categories.

$10,000

How the money works

  • 0% interest, with no monthly payment while you live there
  • Repaid when you sell or refinance — not on a schedule
  • First-time buyer status is not required, which is unusual and worth knowing
  • The home must be inside Ogden city limits — the boundary matters more than people expect

Ogden's city boundary is genuinely confusing. Homes with an Ogden mailing address are frequently outside city limits and therefore ineligible, while some that feel like they're in South Ogden or Washington Terrace are actually inside. Before you fall for a house, it's worth checking the parcel — we do that for free.

What else stacks in Weber County

Own in Ogden is rarely the only thing you qualify for. These are separate programs with separate money.

  • Weber County grant — roughly $5,000, secured as a lien and forgiven entirely after five years of ownership
  • FHLB HELP — a forgivable grant of around $20,000 with a five-year owner-occupancy requirement
  • Utah Housing Corporation FirstHome — assistance up to 6% of the first mortgage, capped at $27,500
  • Federal HOME funds — deferred loans available in parts of the Ogden area
  • FHA financing — 3.5% down with a 580+ credit score, as the base loan underneath any of it

Not every combination is allowed simultaneously, and each has its own price caps and income limits. Sorting out which stack you actually qualify for is a single conversation, and it's worth having before you start touring.

Price caps to keep in mind

Own in Ogden currently applies to homes priced at roughly $430,000 for existing construction and $467,000 for new. Utah Housing's separate cap for Weber County sits near $495,000. Caps are updated periodically, so we confirm the current figures before you write an offer.
