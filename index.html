About GROWV

A different kind of brokerage.

GROWV is a Utah brokerage built on a single belief — that buying or selling a home is one of the most personal decisions a family ever makes, and it deserves to be handled with the same precision and grace as a stay at a great hotel.

Founded and led by broker-owner Jonathan Perez, our practice draws on more than a decade across lending, property management, and strategic negotiation. We’re selective about who we represent and obsessive about how we represent them. Every client receives a direct line to the broker, fluent service in English and Spanish, and a process designed to feel effortless from first showing to final signature.
